# moodle-classroom-audit

Use this skill for classroom monitoring and structural audits: course contents, activity deadlines, participation, completion, and report inputs. It is an analytical flow and may combine several read operations.

## Sequence

1. Resolve the course and connection and confirm the requested audit window.
2. Read the course structure and classify modules by activity type.
3. Read deadlines, completion, participants, and participation evidence only for the requested scope.
4. Reconcile identifiers across results before calculating coverage or risk.
5. Produce an audit with source operations, missing capability warnings, pagination state, and time of collection.

## Classification

Course contents are structural evidence. Assignment, quiz, forum, SCORM, page, resource, URL, and label details remain owned by their domain gateways. The audit may aggregate them but must not duplicate their write or authorization logic.

## Trust rules

Do not label a course inactive from a single empty endpoint. Distinguish unavailable capability, empty result, partial page, and confirmed zero. Reports are generated by the report flow only after the underlying reads are complete and normalized.
